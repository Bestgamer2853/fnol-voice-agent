## Design Context

### Users
An engineer preparing for, or actively answering questions in, a Staff-level AI engineering interview. They need to recall repository-specific facts in seconds without opening the codebase.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, and formidable. The experience should replace interview panic with a sense of command.

### Aesthetic Direction
An offline-first technical field guide: the restraint of Apple and Linear, the documentation clarity of Stripe, and the command-palette speed of Raycast. Use a warm paper surface, ink-forward typography, a deliberate signal color, and an information-dense desktop workspace that remains usable on a phone.

### Design Principles
- Surface verified repository facts first; visibly distinguish current behavior from production improvements.
- Optimize for keyboard-first retrieval and deep links over ornamental UI.
- Make high-probability answers scannable under pressure, with details available one layer deeper.
- Maintain excellent contrast, focus states, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Prefer a small number of meaningful visual cues over card-heavy decoration.
